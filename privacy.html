Privacy Policy

Your family's trust matters to us. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Harmony and Health Microschool ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at harmonyandhealthmicroschool.com (the "Site"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information that you and your family share with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, and safeguard that information when you visit our Site, submit a form, or attend one of our events.

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you give us directly, and information collected automatically when you use the Site.

Information you provide to us. When you fill out a "Request Information" form, an event RSVP form, or otherwise contact us, you may provide:

  • Your name and your contact details, such as email address and phone number
  • Information about your child, such as first name, age, or grade level
  • Any questions, comments, or other details you choose to include in your message

Information collected automatically. When you browse the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • Your device and browser type, operating system, and general location based on your IP address
  • Pages you visit, links you click, and the dates and times of your visits
  • Information collected through cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Google Analytics

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the information you request about our school
  • Register and follow up with you regarding events you RSVP to attend
  • Guide your family through enrollment, tuition, and Florida scholarship questions
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site and our communications
  • Measure the performance of our advertising and understand how visitors find and use our Site

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Site uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how the Site is used, and support our advertising. Specifically, we use:

  • Google Analytics to understand Site traffic and how visitors interact with our pages.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to show relevant ads to people who have visited our Site. You can learn more in Meta's Privacy Policy and adjust your ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings.

Most web browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some parts of the Site may not function as intended.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to operate our school and Site, including with:

  • Service providers that help us run the Site and communicate with families, such as our form providers (for example, JotForm and Kaipod Newton), email and hosting providers, Google, and Meta.
  • Scholarship and education partners, such as Step Up For Students, when you ask us to help with a Florida scholarship application.
  • Legal and safety purposes, when required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and property of our families, our staff, or the public.

Children's Privacy

Our Site and forms are directed to parents and guardians, not to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through this Site. Any information about a child that we receive is provided by a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us information without your consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We keep personal information only for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, support enrollment, and meet our legal and operational needs, and then dispose of it securely.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you, and request that we correct or delete it
  • Opt out of receiving emails from us by replying with "unsubscribe" or contacting us directly
  • Manage cookies through your browser settings and ad preferences through your Facebook, Instagram, and Google accounts

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Third-Party Links

Our Site may link to other websites, such as our social media pages or partner organizations. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
