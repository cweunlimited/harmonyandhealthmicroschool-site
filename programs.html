Our Program

What Your Child Will Learn

A rich, balanced program of personalized academics, an hour of daily music, real movement and wellness, and hands-on exploration, all in a class of just 15 and built around how your child learns best.

Our Week

A Steady, Balanced Rhythm

Monday through Thursday follow a focused rhythm of academics, daily music, and movement. Fridays break from the routine for hands-on, choice-driven exploration. Every core subject runs on a fully accredited, adaptive curriculum, so each child advances by ability rather than grade level, never held back and never rushed.

Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 3:00 PM Kindergarten through 5th grade
Core Academics

The Subjects We Cover

Reading, writing, math, science, and social studies, taught through a personalized, accredited curriculum that meets each child exactly where they are.

Language Arts

Phonics and early reading building to confident, fluent comprehension, plus vocabulary, grammar, spelling, and writing that grows from sentences to stories and essays.

Mathematics

Number sense and arithmetic through fractions, geometry, measurement, and problem-solving, with each child progressing the moment they are ready.

Science

Hands-on discovery of living things, the earth and weather, space, and how matter and energy work, full of questions, observation, and experiments.

Social Studies

Communities, geography, history, civics, and world cultures, helping children understand the world and their place in it.

Optional

Add a Foreign Language

Families can choose to add foreign language study to their child's program. Through our online curriculum, students can learn Spanish, French, German, or American Sign Language at their own pace, an enriching option for curious learners who want to go further.

Every Single Day

More Than Academics

Alongside the core subjects, every day includes the programs that make Harmony and Health Microschool different.

Music

A full hour every day with our Florida-certified music educator: singing, rhythm, instruments, theory, and the pure joy of making music.

Physical Fitness and Wellness

Daily movement, fitness, and coordination with our professional wellness coach, building strength and lifelong healthy habits.

Garden and Nutrition

Tending our garden and learning where food comes from, connecting children to healthy eating in a hands-on way.

Connection and Character

Each day opens with Connection Time, building focus, gratitude, goal-setting, and a warm, respectful classroom community.

Fridays

Exploration Fridays

Fridays look different on purpose. There is no standard curriculum work. Instead, students choose from a rotating lineup of hands-on activities that change every single week. It is choice-driven, but always guided by our educators, so the day stays purposeful, creative, and fun. One week might be a group science project, the next an art studio or a STEM building challenge.

The Right Fit

Who Thrives at Harmony and Health Microschool

Harmony and Health Microschool is built for bright, curious, motivated learners who flourish when they are challenged at their own level. Because our curriculum lets each child advance by ability, gifted and advanced students are never held back, and a class of 15 means every child is truly known.

Our small, focused setting is ideal for children who thrive with personalization and a close, engaged peer group. Because every spot matters, we take the time to get to know each family before enrolling, so we can be confident your child will flourish here.
