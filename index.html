Our Story

A School Built on Two Passions

Harmony and Health Microschool was born from a simple but powerful belief: children thrive when they are seen as individuals, not numbers in a classroom. Founded by a certified music teacher and a professional fitness, health & wellness coach, our school brings together personalized academics, artistic expression, and physical well-being in one nurturing community.

Harmony

Music is a language every child can speak. Our curriculum integrates music education into daily learning, nurturing creativity, discipline, and self-expression from the very beginning.

Health

A healthy body supports a healthy mind. Physical education, nutrition awareness, and wellness habits are woven throughout every school day, not treated as extras.

Learning

Every child's academic journey is uniquely their own. Our personalized, technology-assisted curriculum adapts to each learner's pace, strengths, and individual goals.

Our Approach

Learning That Fits Your Child

Traditional one-size-fits-all education leaves many children behind. At Harmony and Health Microschool, every student receives their own individualized online curriculum that grows with them, challenges them at the right level, and celebrates their unique strengths.

Individualized Assessment

Each student begins with a comprehensive assessment so we understand exactly where they are academically, and chart a personalized path forward.

Personal Online Curriculum

Every child works through a customized digital curriculum tailored to their learning style, level, and pace. Never held back, never left behind.

Music Integration

Music education is woven into the daily experience, building creativity, focus, memory, and discipline alongside core academic learning.

Movement & Wellness

Physical education, nutrition, and wellness habits are part of every school day, helping children show up energized, focused, and ready to learn.

Meet the Educators

Passionate. Qualified. Dedicated.

Harmony and Health Microschool is a family endeavor, built by two educators who share one vision: a school where every child is truly known, valued, and empowered to reach their full potential.

Mrs. Lambe-Estevez

Lead Educator & Music Teacher

Certified through the Florida Department of Education as a K–12 Music Teacher, Mrs. Lambe-Estevez brings 13 years of classroom experience and 26 years of working with children to Harmony and Health Microschool. A professional singer and flutist specializing in Elementary Music Education and Choir Conducting, she guides students in exploring their musical voices through singing, recorder, xylophone, drums, and more. Beyond music, she oversees each student's individualized academic curriculum, bringing the same passion for personalized education to every subject she teaches.

FL DOE Certified K–12 Music Teacher Elementary Music & Choir Professional Singer & Flutist

Mr. Estevez

Health & Wellness Educator

Trained by the National Academy of Sports Medicine in personal training and nutrition coaching, Mr. Estevez has spent 9 years as a full-time fitness, health and wellness professional, with over 24 years dedicated to the study of strength, nutrition, and longevity. He brings that real-world expertise into the classroom, teaching students age-appropriate fitness, healthy eating habits, and wellness practices they will carry with them for life.

NASM-Trained 9 Years Professional Experience PE & Nutrition Educator
Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

We know choosing the right school for your child is one of the most important decisions you'll make. Here are answers to the questions we hear most from families.

A microschool is a small, intentional learning community that blends highly personalized, individually paced learning with the community and structure of a traditional school. Each student receives individual attention and learns at their own pace, within a structured, social, and educator-led environment.

A microschool offers the qualified educators, daily structure, and peer community of a traditional private school, but in a much smaller setting. With only 15 students, every child receives personalized attention and an individualized curriculum that adapts to their level and pace, something large classrooms simply cannot provide.

Each student is assessed individually and assigned their own digital curriculum that adapts to their level, learning style, and pace. No two students follow the exact same path. A child can advance quickly in math while taking more time in reading, without ever being held back or left behind.

Students cycle through personalized academic work, music instruction with Mrs. Lambe-Estevez, and physical education and wellness sessions with Mr. Estevez, all within a structured daily schedule designed to balance focused learning with movement and creativity.

We are intentionally small by design. We accept a maximum of 15 students, and that is a permanent cap rather than a first-year limit. This ensures every child receives genuine individual attention from our educators every single day.

We serve children in Kindergarten through 5th Grade, ages 5 to 11. Each child is assessed upon enrollment so their curriculum begins exactly where they are, regardless of their grade level. To enroll in Kindergarten, your child must turn 5 by September 1st.

Yes. We use a fully accredited curriculum, so your child's education meets rigorous, recognized academic standards from day one. As a private school, we also plan to pursue formal accreditation through one of the established accrediting agencies. Because school accreditation is typically earned over the first few years of operation, we anticipate achieving it after our doors open.
