Tuition

A Premium, Individualized Education

Harmony and Health Microschool delivers what most schools cannot: certified specialists, a class capped at 15, and a full day of academics, music, and wellness built around your child. Here is our tuition, and the Florida scholarship that helps every family afford it.

What Your Tuition Includes

Why Families Choose Harmony and Health Microschool

Tuition reflects a genuinely specialized experience, not a one-size-fits-all classroom. Every dollar goes toward individual attention and expert instruction your child receives every single day.

A Class of Just 15

Our class is permanently capped at 15 students total, so every child is known, challenged, and supported individually, every single day.

Certified Music Education

Daily instruction from a Florida-certified K-12 music educator with 13 years of experience, woven into the core of the school day, not an occasional add-on.

Wellness, Fitness and Nutrition

A professional, NASM-trained wellness coach guides movement, nutrition, and healthy habits daily, building lifelong physical and mental well-being.

A Fully Individualized Curriculum

An accredited, adaptive curriculum meets each child exactly at their level and pace across every core subject, never holding them back or leaving them behind.

Taught by Specialists, Not Aides

Your child learns directly from two credentialed professional educators, not a single generalist supervising an app.

The Whole Child

Academics, music, movement, and mindfulness in one cohesive day, nurturing the mind, body, and character together.

The Investment

Tuition for 2026 to 2027

One all-inclusive tuition for a full day of specialized, individualized education. Florida's school choice scholarship, available to every family, brings the real cost down substantially.

Per Student, Per Year
  • $22,000 Standard tuition per year, all inclusive
  • 10% off Founding family discount. For families who join us in our opening year, and your rate stays locked in for as long as your child is enrolled.
  • $19,800 Your tuition as a founding family
  • $11,800 Your estimated cost after the Florida school choice scholarship most families receive

One Tuition. No Hidden Fees.

Most schools stack separate registration, technology, and supply fees on top of tuition. We have built all of that into one transparent price, so there are no surprise add-ons during the year. The only additional item is a one-time $350 enrollment fee to reserve your child's spot, and it is applied directly toward your tuition rather than charged on top of it.

Families who pay in full receive additional savings, and flexible monthly payment plans and need-based financial aid are available. Reach out and we will walk you through the options that fit your family.

Florida School Choice

Florida Helps Pay for It

Every Florida Family Now Qualifies

Through Florida's Family Empowerment Scholarship (FES-EO) and Florida Tax Credit Scholarship, every K-12 student in Florida is eligible for private education funding, regardless of household income. For most families this is approximately $8,000 per year applied directly toward tuition.

These scholarships are administered by Step Up For Students. We will personally guide your family through the application so the funding is in place before the school year begins.

Scholarship eligibility and award amounts are determined by the State of Florida and Step Up For Students and vary by grade level and circumstance. We are glad to walk through your family's specific numbers in person.
