    Good to Know

    How Membership Works

    These terms apply to everything above: daytime and afternoon classes, Mommy & Me Music, and one-to-one lessons, tutoring and coaching. Straightforward, with nothing hidden in fine print.

    One-hour class sessions

    Every class session is one hour, and each student has no more than two hours of instruction in a day, counting private lessons and coaching. Monday to Thursday, daytime students can add that day's group tutoring session as a third hour.

    One year, eight equal payments

    Membership runs the full year, October through May, and the cost of that year is divided into eight equal monthly payments. Some months hold far fewer class days than others once Thanksgiving, winter break and spring break are taken out. Your payment stays the same every month either way, so you're never guessing the amount due next month.

    Two semesters, two showcases

    The year falls into two semesters, October through January and February through May. That is about the work rather than the paperwork: each semester builds toward its own showcase, and the next one begins with new projects and new repertoire. Your child keeps the same place in the schedule throughout.

    30 days notice to cancel

    Thirty days prior written notice is required to cancel a membership.

    $100 yearly registration per student

    Charged when your child enrolls each year. It covers their class materials for the year and their own Harmony and Health Microschool T-shirt, and applies to daytime and afternoon classes alike.

    Try a class first

    Any single class is $45 as a one-time visit. If you join, that $45 comes off your first payment, so trying costs you nothing extra.
